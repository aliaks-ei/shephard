Shephard UI refresh for 2026

A review of the main mobile screens, with changes ranked by impact and effort. Each change links to the design reference or the research it comes from.

Reviewed on 1 October 2026. I opened 8 screens at 390 px width in mock mode (npm run dev:mock), and I did a dark mode check on Home. I did not test on a real device or with real data.

Keep these

The base is good. Do not rebuild these parts. Refine them.

Floating glass tab barIt already follows the iOS 26 pattern. It needs fewer items, not a new design.
Motion tokens--ease-out-quint and --ease-spring in theme.scss are correct for native feel.
AI category and budget impactuseAICategorization and BudgetImpactCard are strong. The form hides them below a long field list.
Date-grouped activityDay groups with a total per day match the best references.
Privacy eye and pull to refreshBoth are expected in 2026 money apps.

Home

What I saw

  • The greeting "Morning, Demo" is the largest text on the screen. The money figure is second.
  • The hero shows "LEFT TO SPEND" in capitals and a remainder. It does not say if the spend rate is safe.
  • All category bars are teal. Transport is at 100% but it looks the same as the others.
  • Category avatars use Material colours (#4CAF50, #9C27B0). They do not match the teal brand.
  • Expense amounts are grey (rgb(117,117,117)). In dark mode this is 3.7:1 on the card, below the 4.5:1 AA minimum.
  • The "Active plans" and "Templates" links at the bottom repeat the tab bar.
  • In mock data the hero says "11% of €2.500,00 spent". The plan page says "27%" of €975,00. Check this with real data.

Change

  • Make the remainder the largest element. Make the greeting a small caption next to the avatar.
  • Add the daily pace: remaining / daysLeft. Kippo and the Fahmi Nugraha shot both show "you can spend €X a day".KF
  • Add a "today" marker on the hero bar at elapsedDays / totalDays. When the fill passes the marker, show an amber "Ahead of plan" chip.
  • Later: subtract unpaid fixed items (rent, bills) from "left" before the pace math. Copilot Money treats known recurring bills as already spent.9
  • Replace the three teal bars with one stacked bar in category colours and a legend. The Cansaas shot uses this pattern.S
  • Show amounts in ink colour, not grey. Amounts are the main data in a list.
  • Remove the bottom "Active plans" and "Templates" links.

Research: Kaye et al. found that people track money to feel in control and to plan for an unknown future.1 A pace signal answers "am I OK?" directly. Keep the tone neutral: amber for "ahead", never red until the budget is over.

Add expense

What I saw

  • The title "Register New Expense" is formal. The button says "Register Expense".
  • The order is: tabs, receipt photo, a divider "OR ENTER MANUALLY", amount, currency, name, category select, plan select. The amount is a normal-size input.
  • Category is a dropdown. The AI suggestion shows only after the name is typed, and only as a small row.
  • The tabs "Custom Entry" and "Quick Select" use system words, not user words.
  • The sheet opens to full height. NN/g says sheets suit short tasks and a swipe-down can lose the input by accident.23

Change

  • Put the amount first, at 44–48 px, with autofocus and the existing inputmode="decimal". The native decimal keyboard is enough. A custom keypad is not necessary. The Cansaas, Kippo and Fahmi shots all lead with a large amount.SKF
  • Show the currency as a small chip under the amount. Most entries use the default currency.
  • Replace the category dropdown with a chip row: the AI suggestion first (with a sparkle icon), then the 3–4 most used categories, then "More". One tap instead of open, scroll, tap.
  • Show the plan as a chip with the BudgetImpactCard line under it: "€528,00 left in Food after this".
  • Move "Scan receipt" to an icon chip next to the name field. Remove the "OR ENTER MANUALLY" divider.
  • Rename: "Add expense" (title and button), "New" and "From plan" (tabs). The toast then says "Expense added".
  • Block swipe-to-dismiss when the form has input, or ask "Discard expense?" first.

Plans

What I saw

  • Each card shows name, dates, total and "31 days left". It does not show how much is spent.
  • A teal + in the header creates a plan. The + in the tab bar adds an expense. Same icon, different action, on one screen.
  • The subtitle "Manage your financial plans and track your progress" fills space but gives no information.
  • Each card has a kebab menu. This adds a small 24 px target to every card.
  • Completed plans sit in the same list as active plans.

Change

  • Add a thin progress bar and "€267,88 of €975,00" to each card. Add one status chip: "On track", "Ahead of plan" or "€12,40 over".
  • Replace the header + with a text button "New plan". Keep the tab bar + for expenses only.
  • Add a "Plans / Templates" segmented control at the top. Templates leaves the tab bar.
  • Show shared plans with member initials, not only a people icon.
  • Collapse completed plans into a "Completed (2)" group.
  • Move the kebab actions into a long-press or swipe menu, and keep them in the plan's "More" menu.
  • Remove the subtitle.

Plan detail

What I saw

  • Entertainment is at 104%. The row shows a red "Over" badge, "€0.00 left" and a teal bar. The overspend amount is not visible.
  • Bills & Utilities uses a red avatar. Red also means "over budget" on the same screen.
  • "€0.00" uses a dot. All other EUR values use a comma. The cause is utils/currency.ts:25, which returns a fixed string for zero.
  • The summary says "Still to pay". Home says "Left to spend". The rows say "left".
  • The tabs include "Edit". The bottom bar also has "Edit". The bar gives Export and Share the same weight as adding an expense.

Change

  • For over-budget rows, show "€0,98 over" in red and add a red tail past the bar end. The Fahmi Nugraha shot marks the overspend on the bar in the same way.F
  • Colour each category bar in its category colour. Use red only for "over".
  • Use one word: "left". "€708,10 left of €975,00".
  • Tabs: Overview, Items, Activity. Move Edit to the More menu, or show it as a header action for owners.
  • Bottom bar: one wide "Add expense" button, plus Share and More as round glass buttons. Export goes into More.
  • Fix the zero format: run zero through the same toLocaleString path as other values.

Activity

What I saw

  • Rows under the "TODAY" header also show "Oct 1, 2026". The date shows twice.
  • Day headers use capitals ("TODAY", "SEP 29").
  • Amounts are grey and lighter than the item names.
  • The category chips cut off at the right edge with no fade. "Food & Groc" looks broken.
  • The header + and the tab bar + both show here.
  • Swipe actions exist in RecentExpensesList.vue but not on this list.

Change

  • Remove the date from rows inside a day group. Show the plan name only, or category and plan.
  • Use sentence case for day headers: "Today", "Yesterday", "Mon 29 Sep".
  • Show amounts in ink colour with tabular figures.
  • Add an "All" chip first and a fade mask on the chip row edge.
  • Add a small summary on top: month total and a 14-day bar strip. The Keitoto and Morph Studio shots open the history with a summary like this.TM
  • Reuse the q-slide-item swipe from RecentExpensesList.vue. Delete with an undo snackbar, not a confirm dialog.

Templates, Settings, Notifications

Templates

  • Every card shows the caption "Total amount". Remove it. The number explains itself.
  • Show the item count and the category colours as a small stacked bar. This shows what the template contains before you open it.
  • Add a "Start plan" action on each card. Creating a plan is the main reason to open a template.

Settings

  • Theme uses a dropdown for three values. Use a segmented control: Light, Dark, System.
  • The "Sign out" button sits under the tab bar at the end of the scroll. Add bottom padding equal to the tab bar height plus env(safe-area-inset-bottom).
  • An error toast "Unable to load AI connections" showed at the top in mock mode. Show this error inside the AI connections card, not as a toast.
  • Move Settings to the avatar on Home. Remove the gear from the global header.

Notifications

  • The bell badge says 12. The sheet says 6 unread. Check which number is correct.
  • Each row has a red trash icon and a check icon. Six red icons make the list look like an error state. Use swipe to delete and tap to mark as read. Keep "Mark all as read" in the menu.
  • Group by "New" and "Earlier" in sentence case.

Category colours come from categories.color in the database, as Material hex values. You do not need a migration. Normalise them in CSS with relative colour syntax. This keeps the stored hue and sets one lightness and chroma: 

.category-tile {
  --c: var(--category-color);           /* bound from category.color */
  color: oklch(from var(--c) 0.55 0.14 h);
  background: oklch(from var(--c) 0.94 0.03 h);
}
.body--dark .category-tile {
  color: oklch(from var(--c) 0.78 0.12 h);
  background: oklch(from var(--c) 0.30 0.05 h);
}

Relative colour syntax works in Safari 18+, Chrome 119+ and Firefox 128+, to my knowledge. Check your browser support target first. Red categories still need a manual hue change, because red is reserved for "over".

Type

Body text uses the system stack at 14 px. Keep the system stack for body text, because it feels native. Add one display face for money figures and page titles. I suggest Geist: it has tabular figures and a clear comma, and this page uses it. One weight range in WOFF2, subset to Latin, is about 30–40 KB. The service worker can cache it.

  • Raise the base body size from 14 px to 15 px on mobile. iOS body text is 17 pt. 14 px is small for a list of amounts.
  • Remove all capital labels: "LEFT TO SPEND", "TODAY", "OR ENTER MANUALLY", "UNREAD".
  • Show cents smaller than the units in the hero (€708,10). Most references do this.

Shape and surface

  • Use radius for hierarchy: 24 px for the hero and sheets, 20 px for cards, 11 px for icon tiles, full round for chips and buttons. Now buttons and inputs are 6 px and cards are 12 px, which reads as a 2023 web form.
  • Use glass only on the control layer: tab bar, action bar, sheet header. Apple's guidance is the same: glass is for controls that float above content, not for content.4
  • Replace the hero gradient with flat pine. Keep one soft glow at most. Flat dark surfaces read more modern than teal-to-navy gradients in the 2026 references.
  • Respect prefers-reduced-transparency: use the existing --glass-bg-fallback token.

Motion

  • Keep one moment: the hero count-up (useCountUp) on first load. Remove the stagger on other cards. During the walkthrough, the stagger left cards half-faded in some screenshots.
  • Animate the pace bar fill once. Animate a new row into the list after "Add expense", so the user sees where it went.
  • Minimise the tab bar on scroll down and restore it on scroll up. iOS 26 does this.5

Expressive emphasis, used once per screen

Google's Material 3 Expressive research ran 46 studies with more than 18,000 participants. Users found key elements up to 4 times faster when the key action had a bigger size, a stronger colour and a distinct shape. The age gap in visual search also went away.6 For Shephard, the key element is the money figure on Home and the "Add expense" action everywhere. Give those two the emphasis. Keep the rest quiet.

Reach and target size

  • Hoober's field study saw 49% one-handed use. Users also prefer to read and tap near the middle of the screen.7 Keep the hero and the primary list in the middle. Put the primary action at the bottom. Put rare actions (Export, Delete) in menus.
  • Kebab menus and the inline check and trash icons are about 24 px. This meets WCAG 2.2 2.5.8 (24 px minimum) but not Apple's 44 pt.8 Make tap targets 44 px.

Defects found during the walkthrough

  • Zero amounts use the wrong decimal mark"€0.00" shows next to "€25,98". src/utils/currency.ts:25 returns `${symbol}0.00` before the locale format.
  • Teal text fails AA contrast#00919A on white is 3.8:1. Links like "View all" and "See all categories" are 14–15 px, so they need 4.5:1. White text on the teal "Register Expense" button has the same ratio. src/css/theme.scss, --primary
  • Dark mode amounts fail AA contrastrgb(117,117,117) on the #1C1C1C card is 3.7:1. .text-amount in src/css/app.scss
  • Over-budget amount is hidden104% shows "€0.00 left". The user cannot see how much is over. Plan detail category rows
  • Hero and plan totals disagree in mock modeHero: 11% of €2.500,00. Plan: 27% of €975,00. BudgetOverviewCard falls back to plan.total when overview.totalBudget is missing. This may be only a mock data issue. src/components/dashboard/BudgetOverviewCard.vue:138
  • Badge count and unread count differBell badge 12, sheet "Unread 6". Notifications
  • Segmented control artefactAfter a switch to "Quick Select", a white strip from the old indicator stayed visible next to the new one. This may be a mid-animation frame. Add expense sheet tabs
